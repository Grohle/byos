TweetNaCl - public domain statement.

TweetNaCl has no licence file. Its home page, https://tweetnacl.cr.yp.to (read on
2026-11-10), states:

   "TweetNaCl is a self-contained public-domain C library, so it can easily be
   integrated into applications."
